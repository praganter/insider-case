     Fix: in both loops, double the limit before the retry: params.MaxTokens *= 
     2, at the point where retry is true. The retry stays limited to one by
     outcome().

     Test: no key needed. fakeAPI already records request bodies (*[]string,
     anthropic_test.go:43). In TestAnthropicMaxTokensRetriesOnce and
