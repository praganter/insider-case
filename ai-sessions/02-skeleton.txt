     /clear commands examined to understand impact on session archival. 
     Already-exported files (ai-sessions/01-planning.txt) are safe - these 
     commands don't modify existing exports. Risk identified in future exports 
     after /compact or /clear operations may lose or summarize earlier 
     conversation segments.

     Learned: Session export strategy for multi-phase projects: /export writes
     snapshot at invocation time and doesn't retroactively modify saved files. 
     /compact replaces old messages with summaries in active session - 
     subsequent exports may contain only summary of earlier phases plus full 
     recent conversation. /clear removes conversation history entirely - any 
     content not previously exported is permanently lost. Best practice 
     workflow: export before /compact or /clear commands, export after to verify
     what was preserved, number sequentially (01-planning.txt, 02-skeleton.txt,
     etc.). Insider One case study requires complete session transcripts, so 
     export at major milestone completions (phase boundaries) before any
